Experience the Odoo CRM app

Try it yourself in three simple steps. First, click the purple Score Leads button and Odoo will rank your prospects. Each card then shows a percentage chance of becoming a customer, and the most promising ones move to the top of their column. Next, click any card to see why it scored that way and what to do next, such as calling that person today. Finally, use Move to next stage to watch the score change as a deal progresses. This is a demonstration with sample data, so nothing you do here is saved.
